# Stromboli

An island volcano in the Aeolian arc north of Sicily that has been erupting, a few times an hour, for at least 2 000 years. Sailors called it the Lighthouse of the Mediterranean. It gave its name to the eruption style, [[Strombolian eruptions]], and so indirectly to most of the [[Scoria]] on Earth.

![[stromboli-night.jpg|480]]
*Stromboli at night. Each burst throws incandescent scoria and bombs a few hundred metres above the crater. Photo: kuhnmi, CC BY 2.0.*

## The mechanism

Gas bubbles rising through the conduit coalesce into slugs that fill its width. When a slug reaches the surface of the lava column it bursts, throwing the film of melt above it into the air as clots. Each clot quenches into a scoria clast or a bomb. Bursts come every 10 to 20 minutes, with the gas measured on the way up by the volcano observatory.

```
   conduit                     burst
   ┌───┐                       ┌───┐
   │ · │  small bubbles        │ ∴ │  clots in the air
   │ ○ │  coalescing           │╱ ╲│
   │ ◯ │  slug                 │   │  film breaks
   │▓▓▓│  melt                 │▓▓▓│
```

## Paroxysms

The regular activity is harmless to the villages on the island's flanks. The paroxysms are not: on 3 July 2019 a sudden explosion sent a pyroclastic flow down the Sciara del Fuoco and killed a hiker. Access to the summit has been restricted since.

> [!warning] Sciara del Fuoco
> The scar on the north-west flank down which the scoria and bombs roll into the sea. It collapsed on 30 December 2002, producing a tsunami with 10 m run-up on the island.
