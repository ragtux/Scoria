# 2026-06-02 Etna lapilli sampling

## Route

Rifugio Sapienza (1 910 m) → cable car to 2 500 m → 4×4 to Torre del Filosofo area (2 900 m) with the guide → on foot to the 2021 lapilli field at Piano del Vescovo → down by the 2002 flow at Piano Provenzana on the north side in the afternoon.

## Observations

- The 2021 fall deposit is a continuous sheet of black [[Scoria]] lapilli 20–40 mm across, two to three clasts thick on the lee side of the old cones. Still unweathered: glassy, no dust.
- Sorting is remarkable. In a 30 cm pit the deposit is inversely graded, finer at the base, which fits a paroxysm that intensified over its first hour.
- Bomb fragments on the South-East Crater flank with bread-crust surfaces; interiors still vesiculating when the rind froze. See [[Tephra#Bombs]].
- The South-East Crater itself was quiet, a steady white plume. Degassing audible as a jet-engine roar from 500 m.
- The 2002 flow at Piano Provenzana is ʻaʻā with plagioclase phenocrysts to 1 cm, much coarser than the 2021 scoria.
