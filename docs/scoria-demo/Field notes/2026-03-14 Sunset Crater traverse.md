# 2026-03-14 Sunset Crater traverse

## Route

Visitor centre → Lava Flow Trail loop (1.6 km) → Bonito flow margin → Kana-a flow margin by road → Lenox Crater trail for the view back. About 6 km on foot, 09:30 to 15:00.

## Observations

- The cone is sharper than it looks in photographs. Slopes measured with the clinometer at 31°, 32°, 33° on three sides: the angle of repose of loose [[Scoria]], as [[Cinder cone#Anatomy]] says it should be.
- Rim colour: red and yellow bands visible from the trail, oxidised scoria over black. The boundary is sharp, consistent with a final fountaining phase after the cone was built.
- Bonito flow: ʻaʻā with a clinker surface 1–2 m thick over a massive core, exposed where the trail cuts it. Squeeze-ups and a hornito near the trail junction.
- Kana-a flow: narrower, channelled, with [[Tephra|lapilli]] overlying it on the eastern margin. So the tephra kept falling after this flow was emplaced.
- Ash blanket in the pine woods east of the road: 20 cm of black lapilli over red soil, the Sinagua's mulch.
- No climbing on the cone (closed since 1973); all cone samples are from fallen blocks at the base.

> [!warning] Loose scoria
> The trail margin is ankle-deep lapilli. It flows like gravel and every footprint stays. Stay on the boardwalk sections where there are any.
