# Field card

The two pages that go in the sample bag, laminated: the five hand tests that settle a [[Scoria|scoria]] at the outcrop and the numbers from the rock notes on the first page, the logging protocol from [[2026-09-21 Lab density runs|the density runs]] on the second. The PDF is the one source; the notes it was written from are [[Scoria#Field tests]] and [[Vesiculation#Measuring it]].
