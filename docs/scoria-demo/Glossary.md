# Glossary

Short definitions, each with a block id so other notes can embed one line.

**Vesicle** — a cavity in a volcanic rock left by a gas bubble that was trapped as the melt quenched. Vesicles are what make [[Scoria]] and [[Pumice]] light. ^vesicle

**Vesicularity** — the volume fraction of a rock that is vesicles, written Φ. Measured from bulk and dense-rock densities, see [[Vesiculation#Measuring it]]. ^vesicularity

**Tephra** — any fragmental material thrown out of a vent, whatever its size or composition. Sorted by grain size into ash, lapilli and blocks or bombs. See [[Tephra]]. ^tephra

**Lapilli** — tephra between 2 mm and 64 mm across. Most of a [[Cinder cone]] is lapilli-sized [[Scoria]]. ^lapilli

**Cinder** — the field name for lapilli-sized scoria. "Cinder cone" and "scoria cone" are the same landform. ^cinder

**Dense-rock equivalent (DRE)** — the density the rock would have with every vesicle closed, used as the denominator in vesicularity. ^dre

**Strombolian** — a mildly explosive eruption style in which gas slugs burst at a lava surface and throw out incandescent scoria and bombs. Named after [[Stromboli]]. ^strombolian

**Monogenetic** — a volcano that erupts once, over months to years, and never again. Cinder cones are the commonest kind; [[Parícutin]] is the best observed one. ^monogenetic

**Pāhoehoe and ʻaʻā** — the two surface textures of basaltic lava flows: ropy and smooth, or clinkery and rough. Both are Hawaiian words, both are used everywhere. ^pahoehoe

**Devitrification** — the slow conversion of volcanic glass to crystals. It is why old [[Obsidian]] is rare and old [[Rhyolite]] is not. ^devitrification
