# Basalt

Basalt is the commonest volcanic rock on Earth and the parent of almost everything in this vault. [[Scoria]] is frothed basalt, [[Reticulite]] is basalt foam, and the [[Columnar jointing|columns]] at the Giant's Causeway are basalt that cooled slowly and shrank.

![[columnar-basalt-daguoye.jpg|560]]
*Columnar basalt at Daguoye, Taiwan, under the Milky Way. Photo: Mark Kao, CC0.*

## Composition

45 to 52 % SiO₂, rich in iron and magnesium. The mineral assemblage is plagioclase feldspar, clinopyroxene and often olivine, in a fine groundmass that may be partly glass. It erupts at 1100 to 1250 °C with a [[Magma viscosity|viscosity]] of 10 to 100 Pa·s, which is why it flows for tens of kilometres and fountains rather than explodes.

## Flow textures

- **Pāhoehoe** — smooth, ropy, billowy surfaces on fluid, gas-poor flows.
- **ʻAʻā** — a rubble of [[Scoria|scoriaceous]] clinker over a massive core, on cooler and faster flows.
- **Pillow** — bulbous lobes with glassy rinds, from flows that entered water.

## Where

Everywhere there is a mid-ocean ridge, a hotspot or a rift: the ocean floor, [[Kīlauea]], Iceland, the Deccan and Columbia River flood basalts, and the cinder fields of [[Sunset Crater|Arizona]] and [[Parícutin|Michoacán]]. [[Mount Etna]] erupts a potassium-rich basalt that grades into [[Andesite|trachybasalt]].
